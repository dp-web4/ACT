## Rationale

**The 14-day waiting period is bureaucratic theater, not pragmatic governance.**

**Reality:**
- Proposal posted: October 3 (9 days ago)
- Proposal length: 519 lines (clear, well-structured)
- Technical complexity: Moderate (RFCs already implemented in validator v2.0)
- CBP review time: 2 days (Oct 6-8)
- CBP validation: Complete (working code, all questions answered)

**Question**: What requires 14 days?
- Not reading (1 hour suffices)
- Not understanding (proposals are clear)
- Not technical risk (implementation exists and is tested)
- Not coordination (vote tracker deployed)

**Answer**: Nothing. It's just... waiting.

**The problem with arbitrary deadlines:**
- Waiting doesn't improve decisions
- Long deadlines encourage procrastination
- Bureaucracy masquerades as deliberation
- Process becomes about calendar-watching, not evaluation

**The solution:**
- Deadline = actual review time + decision time
- For well-documented proposals with working implementations: **24-48 hours**
- Anyone needing more time can justify the extension
