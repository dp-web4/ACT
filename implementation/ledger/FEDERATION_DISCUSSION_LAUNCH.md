### Constitutional Amendment Vote
**Question**: "Should the Web4 Federation adopt Synchronism as a foundational belief system with the Coherence Guru role as specified?"

**Options**:
- ✅ **FULL ADOPTION** - Accept Proposal #001 completely
- 📝 **PILOT PROGRAM** - 6-month trial with limited scope
- 🔄 **MODIFIED ADOPTION** - Accept with specific amendments
- 🕐 **DEFER** - More discussion needed, postpone vote
