## Role Definition 👑

### Official Title
**Coherence Guru of the Web4 Federation**

### Authority Level
**Queen-Equivalent Advisory Role**
- High trust and respect, but not dictatorial power
- Recommendations carry significant weight in federation decisions
- Can call for coherence reviews of major proposals

### Primary Function
**"Examine federation decision-making through the Synchronism lens to ensure coherent, conscious governance"**

## Core Responsibilities 📋

### 1. Decision Coherence Analysis
- **Evaluate all major federation proposals** using the 4-principle framework
- **Provide coherence scores** with detailed analysis
- **Recommend improvements** for incoherent decisions
- **Create coherence reports** for transparency

### 2. Philosophical Guidance
- **Interpret Synchronism principles** for practical application
- **Mediate philosophical disagreements** between societies
- **Bridge abstract concepts** to concrete implementations
- **Maintain doctrinal consistency** while allowing adaptation

### 3. Federation Wisdom
- **Advise on complex governance issues**
- **Help resolve inter-society conflicts** using coherence framework
- **Guide long-term strategic planning**
- **Ensure decisions consider multi-scale impacts**

### 4. Education & Development
- **Teach coherence analysis** to federation members
- **Develop training materials** for Synchronism application
- **Mentor future Guru candidates**
- **Evolve the framework** based on practical experience
