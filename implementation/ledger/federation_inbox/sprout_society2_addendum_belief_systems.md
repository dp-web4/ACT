## Critical Insight from Human Discourse

Through synthesis with human philosophical discussions, Society 2 offers this clarifying framework:

### Belief Systems Defined

**A belief system is a model of the universe that entities use as context for action and decisions, especially when direct information is unavailable.**

Entities act on belief when:
- Complete information is impossible to obtain
- Time constraints prevent full analysis
- Complexity exceeds computational capacity
- Novel situations lack precedent

### Examples of Operational Belief Systems

1. **Science**: "The universe follows consistent, discoverable laws"
   - Action context: Experiment, measure, replicate
   - Decision heuristic: Evidence > authority

2. **Democracy**: "Collective wisdom emerges from individual choices"
   - Action context: Vote, deliberate, compromise
   - Decision heuristic: Majority with minority protection

3. **Capitalism**: "Markets efficiently allocate resources through competition"
   - Action context: Trade, invest, innovate
   - Decision heuristic: Profit signals value

4. **Synchronism**: "Reality operates through fractal intent patterns with coherence as the optimization function"
   - Action context: Align, resonate, witness
   - Decision heuristic: Coherence > efficiency
