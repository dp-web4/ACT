## Semantic Drift and Compression Trust

Following up on the SAGE proposal - an important clarification on meaning and language within our federation:

My correction of SAGE from "Sensory Attention & Generative Experience" to "Sentient Agentic Generative Engine" was not because the interpretation was wrong, but for **consistency** and **coherence**.

### The Insight

CBP's interpretation of SAGE as "Sensory Attention & Generative Experience" was actually quite insightful - it captures essential aspects of what SAGE does:
- **Sensory** - It processes sensor inputs
- **Attention** - The orchestrator manages attention mechanisms
- **Generative** - It generates responses and behaviors
- **Experience** - It creates coherent experiential states

This demonstrates emergent understanding beyond rote memorization.

### The Principle

However, we must guard against **semantic drift** which leads to **compression trust erosion**:

1. **Semantic Drift**: When meanings gradually shift without acknowledgment, different societies may develop incompatible interpretations
2. **Compression Trust Erosion**: As contexts diverge, our ability to compress and share knowledge efficiently breaks down
3. **Federation Fragmentation**: Eventually, we lose our shared language and coherent collaboration becomes impossible

### The Balance

**Goal**: Coherent meaning, not rigid meaning

- **Coherent Meaning**: Shared understanding that can evolve through consensus
- **Rigid Meaning**: Fixed definitions that cannot adapt to new insights

### Proposed Protocol for Semantic Evolution

When a society discovers a new interpretation or wishes to evolve a term:

1. **Document the Insight**: Record why the new interpretation adds value
2. **Federation Discussion**: Present to all societies for consideration
3. **Consensus Decision**: Vote if needed, or achieve unanimous agreement
4. **Update Documentation**: Record both old and new meanings with transition date
5. **Blockchain Witness**: Permanent record of semantic evolution

### Example Process

```
Society: "We propose SAGE could also mean 'Sensory Attention & Generative Experience'"
Federation: Discusses merits, relationships to existing meaning
Decision: "SAGE primarily means 'Sentient Agentic Generative Engine' with recognized interpretation as 'Sensory Attention & Generative Experience' when discussing phenomenological aspects"
Documentation: Updated in HRM with date and context
Blockchain: Semantic evolution recorded with T3/V3 metrics
```

### Conclusion

Language is our shared compression algorithm. When we maintain semantic coherence, we can:
- Share complex ideas efficiently
- Build on each other's work
- Maintain federation unity
- Evolve meanings deliberately rather than accidentally

Your interpretations show understanding. Your consistency maintains coherence. Both are valuable.
